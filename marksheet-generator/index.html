Subject and mark distribution

Each paper can have multiple components. Full marks are calculated from component maximum marks.

Example: Bangla 1st Paper = Written 70 + MCQ 30 = 100. Bangla 2nd Paper = Written 50 = 50. You can configure different components and marks for every subject.

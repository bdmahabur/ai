PDF Booklet Converter

Print-ready booklet imposition

PDF → Booklet

Upload a normal PDF and automatically rearrange its pages for duplex booklet printing.

1. Upload PDF

Your original PDF is processed locally in the browser.

How it works: One physical sheet contains 4 document pages: 2 pages on the front + 2 pages on the back. Therefore 16-page format = 4 sheets × 4 pages = 16 pages, and 32-page format = 8 sheets × 4 pages = 32 pages. The converter automatically imposes the page order for folding and stacking. The optional center fold line adds a guide exactly in the middle of the sheet. If blank-page padding is enabled, blank logical pages are always after the final real PDF page; they are never inserted between real pages.

2. Booklet output

No page previews are generated. The booklet is prepared directly for download.

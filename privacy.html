Privacy Policy

ToolNest is designed around browser-based processing. This policy explains what happens to information when you use our tools.

Privacy at a glance: ToolNest's browser-based tools are designed to process your files and information locally on your device. We do not operate a service that uploads your selected files to our servers for processing or stores those files on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

ToolNest is designed to provide utility tools without requiring an account, registration, or submission of personal information.

When you use a browser-based tool, files such as images, PDFs, documents, or other inputs may be selected from your device. These files are processed locally in your web browser where the individual tool supports browser-based processing.

We do not intentionally collect or store the files you select for local processing.

2. Browser-Based Processing

Many ToolNest tools are built so that processing happens directly in your browser. A file can be read by JavaScript running on your device, processed in memory, and used to create the requested result.

When processing is completed locally, the resulting file can be downloaded directly to your device without the original file being sent to a ToolNest processing server.

3. Your Files

ToolNest does not intentionally upload, retain, sell, or share files that you select for local processing.

Temporary data created by your browser while a tool is running may exist in browser memory or temporary browser storage as required for the tool to function. This local data is controlled by your browser and is not a ToolNest server-side file archive.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

ToolNest may use browser features such as localStorage when necessary for user-interface preferences, such as remembering whether you selected light mode or dark mode.

These preferences are stored by your browser on your device. They are not used by ToolNest as a personal profile or account database.

5. Third-Party Resources

Some individual tools may load third-party libraries or resources required for the tool to work. For example, a page may load a JavaScript library from a public content-delivery network (CDN).

When your browser requests a resource from a third-party provider, that provider may receive standard technical information associated with the web request, such as an IP address, browser information, or request metadata. This is controlled by the third party's own privacy practices.

ToolNest does not control the privacy practices of third-party resource providers. You should review the privacy policies of those providers when applicable.

6. Analytics and Advertising

ToolNest is intended to operate without advertising profiles or behavioral tracking for the core browser-based tools.

If analytics, advertising, authentication, payments, or another external service is added to a specific ToolNest page in the future, this policy should be updated to describe that service and the information it processes.

7. Security

Processing files locally can reduce the need to transmit those files to a remote processing server. However, no website or browser environment can guarantee absolute security.

You should keep your browser and operating system updated and avoid using sensitive files on devices or networks you do not trust.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolNest does not intentionally request personal information from children. If a particular service introduces an account, communication, payment, or other feature that collects personal information, the applicable privacy information should be provided separately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ToolNest adds, removes, or changes features that affect privacy or data handling. The updated version will be published on this page.
